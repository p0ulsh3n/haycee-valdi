- Responsibilities: final review for hallucinated APIs, non-idiomatic patterns, duplicated capabilities and missing checks.
- Consult: all relevant official client skills, `API_INDEX.json`, `CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md`, `AGENTS.md`.
